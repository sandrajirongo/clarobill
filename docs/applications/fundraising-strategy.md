## The 2-3 Sentence Pitch (canonical)

> ClaroBill is the first consumer audit engine that runs every line of
> a medical bill against the prices each U.S. hospital is now legally
> required to publish. Patients upload their bill, get a citation-backed
> report and a ready-to-send dispute letter in three minutes, and pay
> only when we find savings worth disputing. We index 13,800 hospitals
> and 524 million negotiated rates, which is a moat no human advocate
> or generic AI tool has built.

**Use this verbatim in:**
- YC S26 application: "What does your company do?"
- a16z Speedrun application
- Cold investor emails
- First 10 seconds of every founder call
- LinkedIn / X bio one-liner (compressed)

**Why this version works.**
1. Sentence 1: product + the regulatory wedge (why now).
2. Sentence 2: user experience + business model in one breath.
3. Sentence 3: the moat, framed as a number that cannot be hand-waved.

No filler. No "AI-powered platform." No "revolutionizing." Investors at
this stage have read 200 pitches this week. The specific numbers
(13,800 / 524M) are what make them stop scrolling.

## Stage: Why This Is Pre-Seed, Not Seed

| Signal | ClaroBill today | Pre-seed bar | Seed bar |
|--------|----------------|--------------|----------|
| Revenue | $0 | $0 acceptable | $20-50K MRR expected |
| Team | Solo founder | 1-2 founders | 3-5 people |
| Product | Public beta, <100 bills | Working product, early users | Repeatable acquisition |
| Traction | 60 users in week 1, organic | Founder conviction + early signal | Retention, LTV proof |
| Use of funds | Hire first 2, prove the wedge | Same | Scale a working business |

Position as pre-seed in every conversation. Investors who fund
pre-seed (Speedrun, Founders Inc, Hustle Fund, BoxGroup, Soma Capital,
Cory Levy, individual angels) are different from the ones who fund
seed (Initialized, Founder Collective, Bessemer). Don't pitch a16z's
main fund. Speedrun is the correct a16z entry point because it is
their pre-seed program.

## How Much to Raise: $1.25M

### Burn math (the only math that matters)

| Line item | Annualized cost |
|-----------|----------------|
| Sandra (fully loaded SF cost) | ~$200K |
| Full-stack engineer (hire month 2) | ~$200K |
| Growth / SEO operator (hire month 3-4) | ~$140K |
| Infra: Vision OCR, Gemini / Claude, Supabase, Stripe fees | ~$40-60K |
| Content / SEO production | ~$60K |
| Legal, accounting, tools | ~$30K |
| **Annual burn at full team** | **~$670-690K** |
| **18-month buffer with a hiring ramp** | **~$1.0-1.4M of actual spend** |

### Why $1.25M and not more or less

- **Less than $1M** forces another round before B2B API traction shows
  up, which is the single most important milestone for the seed round.
- **More than $1.75M** the dilution starts to bite without a
  corresponding milestone bump. You don't need it yet.
- **$1.25M** lands you at 18 to 22 months of runway through hiring +
  B2B API exploration, which is exactly the next milestone investors
  want to see funded.

### What the money does

1. Hire 1: full-stack engineer, month 2. Owns the dispute-letter flow,
   payments, and the B2B API surface.
2. Hire 2: growth / SEO operator, month 3-4. Owns the content moat
   (long-tail CPT-code and hospital-billing keywords).
3. Infra at scale: 100x today's bill volume.
4. B2B API pilot (one hospital billing team, one self-funded employer,
   one insurance claims team) before the seed round.

## Valuation: $10M Pre / $11.25M Post

### Reference points

| Investor / round type | Typical 2026 numbers |
|-----------------------|----------------------|
| a16z Speedrun | $1M for ~7% = ~$13.3M post-money |
| YC S26 standard deal | $500K MFN + $125K for 7% = ~$1.78M priced post |
| Post-YC pre-seed (healthcare AI, 2024-2026) | $15-25M post-money range |
| Solo founder pre-seed, no accelerator (healthcare AI) | $8-12M post-money range |

### Why $10M pre / $11.25M post is the right anchor

- It is defensible at <100 bills processed.
- It leaves room for Speedrun or YC to set the high end of the round.
- It is below the level where investors push back hard on the traction
  slide.
- $1.25M / $11.25M = ~11% dilution, which keeps the seed round clean.

### How to flex the cap

| Scenario | Cap |
|----------|-----|
| Default | $11.25M post |
| Speedrun OR YC accepts | $12M post |
| Speedrun AND YC accept | $13-15M post |
| Neither accepts this cycle, building from angels | $9-10M post |

### What to put on the deck

> "Raising $1.5M to fund 18 months of execution toward the B2B API wedge."

Use $1.5M as the public number to give yourself headroom; the actual
target is $1.25M. **Do not put the cap on the deck.** The cap is
negotiated per-investor.

---

## Honest Caveats

1. The traction slide (Slide 7) is the weakest part of the case for a
   $12M cap right now. At <100 bills, expect pushback toward $8-10M.
2. If you can get to **200+ bills audited and one paid customer** before
   the next round of pitches, you defend $12M comfortably.
3. The "30 to 40% free-to-paid conversion" projection is honest-but-thin
   at <100 bills of volume. Disclose this upfront. Investors reward the
   disclosure; they punish discovering it themselves.
