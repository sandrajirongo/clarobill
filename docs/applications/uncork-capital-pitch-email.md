**Word count:** ~290 words

Uncork (Jeff Clavier, Andy McLoughlin) is a seed-stage fund with a
pattern of backing technical products that have a consumer-feeling
wedge and an infrastructure tail (Postmates, Fitbit, SendGrid, Front,
Fastly, LaunchDarkly). They are not a healthcare-specialist fund,
which is intentional in this pitch: lead with the wedge shape they
recognize, not the vertical.

---

## Subject line

> ClaroBill — first consumer product on 524M published hospital rates

Why this subject: front-loads the company name (the inbox routes by
company), front-loads the moat number (the only thing in their inbox
they haven't seen 200 times this week), and the word "consumer"
signals the wedge shape Uncork knows how to evaluate.

## What makes this stand out (annotated)

1. **Open with a specific dollar finding, not a company description.**
   The $850 → $96.84 → $753.16 sequence is concrete and unforgettable.
   Most pitches open with "ClaroBill is an AI-powered platform that..."
   This one opens with a number that does the selling.

2. **The moat is a data point, not an adjective.** "13,800 hospitals
   and 524M rates" beats "comprehensive pricing database" every time.

3. **Volunteer the weakness.** "<100 bills processed (the conversion
   data is thin and I won't pretend otherwise)" disarms the
   investor's biggest objection before they raise it. Honesty signal
   that almost no cold email has.

4. **No claim of "AI-powered."** Inverts the trend. "No AI inference
   on the audit itself" is a more sophisticated technical pitch than
   the LLM-everywhere noise in the inbox.

5. **Specific fit thesis.** "Postmates / SendGrid / Fastly pattern"
   shows you've done your homework on Uncork. They will recognize
   it. Don't pitch healthcare specialists with a Postmates analogy
   and don't pitch Uncork with a vertical-healthcare frame.

6. **Time-boxed ask.** "Twenty minutes if the angle interests you."
   No "I'd love to chat." Calibrated, low-friction, implies you have
   other meetings.

7. **The deck and the live product are both linked.** Live products
   are the strongest possible signal at pre-seed. Most cold emails
   only attach a deck.

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] Replace `[link to PDF]` with the actual deck URL (Notion / Drive
      / Pitch link, NOT an attachment — pitches@ inboxes filter heavy
      attachments).
- [ ] Verify clarobill.com/demo is live and the demo loads in <5
      seconds. If it isn't, replace with a Loom recording.
- [ ] Send from sandra@clarobill.com (not a personal Gmail). Custom
      domain signals a real company.
- [ ] Plain-text email. No HTML formatting, no images embedded, no
      tracking pixels (Uncork's filters will mark tracked emails).
- [ ] Send Tuesday or Wednesday morning, 9-10am Pacific. Not weekends,
      not Friday afternoon.
- [ ] Do not BCC other VCs. If they catch it (they will), you're done.
- [ ] Do not follow up before 7 days. If no response after 10 days,
      one short follow-up: "Bumping this in case it got buried. Two
      updates since Monday: [X] and [Y]." Then drop it.
