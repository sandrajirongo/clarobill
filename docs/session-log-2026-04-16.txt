DISCUSSION: What is payer/plan data used for?
================================================================================

User asked to clarify before building.

The three charge benchmarks in priority order:
  1. Medicare rate (PRICE_OUTLIER) — population average, weakest claim
  2. Hospital gross charge (HOSPITAL_CHARGE_OUTLIER) — hospital's own list price
  3. Negotiated rate (NEGOTIATED_RATE_EXCEEDED, planned) — your insurer's exact contract

NEGOTIATED_RATE_EXCEEDED:
  Query: hospital_charges WHERE hospital_npi = ? AND payer_name LIKE ? AND plan_name LIKE ?
  Flag:  billed > negotiated_charge × 1.1
  Claim: "Your insurer contracted $142 for this procedure. You were billed $850."
  This is the most defensible claim — hardest for provider to contest.
